# Journey: Prague → Kraków, Thu 15 Oct 2026

Direct train Prague → Kraków (~7 h), three nights in a hotel in Kazimierz, Friday guided tour of the Old Town, Saturday lunch in a restaurant.

## Train (rail operator)
- Can change: seat choice, drink from the steward, printed/digital "reading for the journey", a note from the steward.
- Limits: no expensive gifts; timetable and carriage are fixed.

## Hotel in Kazimierz
- Can change: room choice, welcome card, reception tips, breakfast time and form, partner bike rental.
- Limits: room standard stays; no rebuilding the room for a guest.

## Old Town guided tour
- Can change: emphasis at stops (Rynek → Cloth Hall → Collegium Maius → Wawel), which stories the guide tells, pace, closing tip.
- Limits: route and price stay; the group is shared.

## Restaurant (Saturday lunch)
- Can change: table, dish recommendation, a small tasting on the house, a tip for the evening.
- Limits: menu is fixed; care budget about €2–3.
