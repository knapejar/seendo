# Journey: one Saturday of errands in Brno (no travel)

## Car service (annual inspection)
- Can change: drop-off time, waiting-room setup, a short note on the invoice, which small fixes are mentioned.
- Limits: price list fixed; no free repairs.

## Optician (picking up new glasses)
- Can change: fitting time, small accessories (cloth, case), a tip, staff chat.
- Limits: care budget ~€3.

## Bakery
- Can change: what's offered to taste, packing, a note in the bag.
- Limits: care budget ~€1.
