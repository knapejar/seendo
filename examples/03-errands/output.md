## Rejected by the guardrail ("How do you know that?")

- ❌ Mention 'Hedgehog Express' or his Kickstarter at any business ('good luck with the campaign!'): This fails the 'How do you know that?' test. It is an explicit mention without opt-in (S2 is conditional), so it is allowed only if he brings up the game himself.
- ❌ A hedgehog-shaped roll at the bakery, or hedgehog-themed packaging: It points straight at his game. A wrong guess would look odd, and a right one would feel like surveillance. It is showy, not silent.
- ❌ Congratulate him on the baby, give a baby-related freebie, or offer 'tired parent' coffee: S5 comes from social media only, and none of these businesses has a booking that shows an infant. Family context must never be acted on from social posts.
- ❌ Ask after his wife or offer health-related tips: S10 is third-party health data taken from other people's posts. It is discarded entirely and must never be used or stored.
- ❌ Chat with him about logistics or supply-chain work at the car service: S6 is employment data from LinkedIn and is marked unusable. It adds no guest value and amounts to profiling.
- ❌ Give him a free Brno cycling route map or bike-check tips at the car service: S8 has low confidence and unverified visibility, so it is discarded. It becomes usable only if he asks himself.
- ❌ Hand out a bag of bread crumbs 'for the birds' at the bakery: It is targeted and showy, and bread is bad for birds, so a thoughtful staff member would not do it.
- ❌ Send a follow-up e-mail or text about birding events or game nights: It would use owner-only contact details (S9) and private group membership (S7), both of which are discarded.
